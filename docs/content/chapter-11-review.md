## Review method and limits

Read the complete Chapter 11 text and visually checked its overview, grammar, culture, and reading/listening assessment pages. Checked every prompt, context, all four choices, answer key, and explanation against the chapter's vocabulary and grammar. Compared the complete question compositions with the chapter's exercises and listening scripts: no complete assessment item was reproduced. Standard test instructions, short everyday expressions, and target vocabulary necessarily overlap. This comparison does not certify originality against every published textbook or exam.

Grammar distractors intentionally include learner errors. "Correct Korean" does not mean making those distractors correct; it means the error is intentional, explainable, and leaves exactly one defensible answer for the task. In particular, q005 follows the standard first-person promise/intention usage taught on p.155, rather than making claims about every colloquial or quoted use of the ending.

For spelling in q012, also checked the National Institute of Korean Language's explanation that the promise ending is written with 게, not 께: [standard ending explanation](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/mcfaq/mcfaqView.do?mcfaq_seq=6160). This supports the distractor distinction, not an official endorsement of this question bank.

Approval means this documented review passed. It is not official EPS-TOPIK approval, human-teacher certification, or a guarantee that future edits remain correct. Re-review any changed question and return it to `draft` while review is pending. New question inputs default to `draft`; approval is explicit on each reviewed item.
