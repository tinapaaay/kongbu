## Review method and limits

Read the whole chapter: vocabulary (pp.171, 174), grammar (pp.172, 175), conversations (pp.173, 176), culture (p.177), and the reading and listening checks with their scripts (pp.178–179). Checked every prompt, context, choice, key, and explanation for correct Korean, a single defensible answer, and Korean a learner who has finished Chapters 1–12 can read.

**Level limit.** Contexts use this chapter's words and grammar plus what earlier chapters teach (-아서/어서, past tense, -(으)ㄹ게요, -아/어야 되다). 성당 and 미사 appear only in the culture note, so q024 is rated harder.

**Originality.** Compared each question with the book's exercises and test items, then avoided their compositions:
- No "which word goes with 슬프다" item (p.178), and no blank whose answer is 바쁩니다 after 일이 많아서 (p.178).

- No "여기는 사원입니다", "오늘도 바빠요?", or "일요일마다 어디에 가요?" items (p.179 listening).
- The conversation lines "어제 일을 많이 해서 피곤해요" and "일이 많아서 친구를 못 만났어요" (p.176) are not reused. q002 and q021 were reworded during review for this reason.

This review does not certify originality against every published textbook or exam.
